# Construct Map

This map separates the Q10 security/control construct from the Q11 trust outcome. They are not interchangeable, and they should not both be described generically as trust.

| Public variable | Source | Item meaning | Item count | Scoring | Model role | Main model? |
|---|---|---|---:|---|---|---|
| bank_service | Q10 | Reliability, ease of use, responsiveness, problem-solving ability, and natural interaction | 5 | Row mean when at least 4 items are valid | Predictor in Models A, B, C; covariate in adjusted Mediation B | Yes |
| bank_security_control | Q10 | Security perception and perceived control | 2 | Row mean only when both items are valid | Predictor in Models A, B, C; moderator construct; mediator in exploratory Mediation A | Yes |
| bank_satisfaction | Q11 | Overall satisfaction with the interaction experience | 1 | Single item | Outcome in Model A; predictor/mediator in later models | Yes |
| bank_trust_outcome | Q11 | Trust that the chatbot can provide accurate and safe service | 1 | Single item | Supplementary descriptive outcome only | No |
| bank_future | Q11 | Future intention to use the chatbot for simple banking tasks | 1 | Single item | Outcome in Model B, Moderation, and mediation models | Yes |
| mox_support | Q16 | Support for the proposed Mox chatbot module | 1 | Reverse-coded as 6 - raw score | Outcome in Model C | Yes |
| ai_attitude | Q5 | General attitude toward new AI technologies or intelligent tools | 1 | Original 1-5 coding | Moderator variable | Yes |

`bank_security_control` is the composite of the Q10 security perception and perceived control items. `bank_trust_outcome` is the separate Q11 single-item trust outcome.
